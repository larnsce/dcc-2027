# DCC 2027 submission

Workshop submission to the Data Curation Conference 2027 ("Don't ghost your data or it will haunt you"), Research Data Management Network, University of Basel, 14 January 2027.

## Contents

- `abstract-workshop.md` - Workshop submission (30 to 50 min), title and abstract to come
- `references/` - Call for proposals (local only, gitignored)

## Submission

- Title plus abstract, max. 350 words
- Must name: discipline, use case, data curation aspect(s) in focus
- By email to researchdata@unibas.ch, subject line "Submission DCC"
- Due date (extended): 21 October 2026
- Notification: 15 November 2026
- Accepted presentations go on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0
